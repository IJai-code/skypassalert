Privacy

Nothing about you leaves your phone.

What we don’t have

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email list.
  • No cookies, no ads, no analytics, no third-party trackers or fonts.
  • No server of our own: the site is plain files on GitHub Pages.

Your location

If you tap “Use my location”, your browser gives the position to the page, the page rounds it to about 100 m, and every prediction is worked out on your phone. It is never sent anywhere. City search runs on a list of cities that comes with the site.

What your browser keeps

So the page opens on your place next time, it saves on this device:

  • spa:place: the place you picked (name, latitude, longitude, time zone).
  • spa:family: bedtime and “simple words”.
  • spa:elements: a copy of the orbit data, for offline use.
  • spa:night: red light on or off, until you close the tab.

To remove all of it, clear this site’s data in your browser settings. Photos you take in the Sky tab stay on your phone unless you choose to share them.

What the page downloads

Only files from skypassalert.com itself: the app, its fonts, orbit data (refreshed every 6 hours from CelesTrak by our build, not by your phone), the city list and a world map. GitHub, which hosts the files, keeps its own server logs; see GitHub’s privacy statement.
