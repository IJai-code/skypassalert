SkyPassAlert

This page needs a newer phone or browser.

It works out exactly when the space stations fly over you, and that math needs a browser from the last few years.

Open it on almost any phone from 2017 on: an iPhone 8 or newer with a recent update, or an Android phone with Chrome. Scan the code with that phone’s camera, or type

skypassalert.com

What you’ll get there:

  • The exact time the International Space Station or China’s Tiangong station flies over where you are tonight.
  • Where to look in plain words: which way to face, how high, how bright, how long.
  • Free. No app to install, no account, no ads, nothing about you collected.

Example of an answer (not a real prediction): Tonight 7:32 PM. Face where the sun went down, a little to the left. It climbs almost straight overhead. Very bright, about 4 minutes.
